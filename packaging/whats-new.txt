Settings fit on one page now. Presets sit at the top beside the pen, so you pick one and adjust it in one place. Right-click a preset to rename it, update it, give it a shortcut or delete it. Opacity, smoothing and the switches you set once are under More.

Shape snapping forgives more. A circle, box or triangle still snaps when its end stops short of where you started or runs on past it, and a box that stops just short of a corner closes at the corner. New installs start with snapping at Lenient, smoothing at Medium and a 1.2 second fade.

Pink joins the colours.

Two shortcuts are gone. Next preset still goes round every preset, so Previous preset isn't needed, and Quit stays in the tray menu and the panel. Neon is gone too: anything that used it draws with Glow.

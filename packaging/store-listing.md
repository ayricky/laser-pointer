Text for Partner Center (Store listings → English (United States)). Limits are Partner Center's.

**Product name:** Laser Pointer

**Short description** (shown in some Windows 10 layouts, ≤ 270 characters)

> A laser pointer for your screen. Draw over slides, code, a browser or a call; the trail fades
> by itself a second later. Rough shapes snap clean, Alt keeps lines straight, and clicks still
> reach the app underneath.

**Description** (≤ 10,000 characters)

> Point at what matters. Press Alt+L and draw over anything on your screen: a slide, a
> spreadsheet, code in a review, a product demo, a video call. The laser trail tapers and fades
> by itself a second later, so the screen is always ready for the next point.
>
> Draw a rough circle, box, triangle or arrow and it settles into a clean one as you let go. Hold
> Alt for a perfectly straight horizontal line, Shift+Alt for a vertical one. Hold Ctrl as you
> let go to keep a stroke freehand.
>
> Switch to the Highlighter or the Pen when you want marks to stay: with Keep strokes on, they
> stay put, even with the laser off and your clicks going through them, until Alt+X clears them.
> Undo and redo anything.
>
> Right-click clicks the app underneath, and the scroll wheel and your slide clicker keep
> working, so you never have to turn the laser off to drive your presentation.
>
> Make it yours: any colour; solid, glow or neon; thickness, opacity, fade and trail length;
> eight animated cursors; up to eight presets; every shortcut can be changed or turned off.
>
> It's tiny and native: under half a megabyte, no CPU while nothing moves, every monitor's
> scaling handled. It sits in the notification area until you need it. No account, no
> telemetry, no network connections.
>
> Laser Pointer is also free and open source (MIT) at github.com/ayricky/laser-pointer. Buying it
> here gets you the signed, self-updating version and supports its development.

**What's new in this version:** First release.

**Product features** (≤ 20, ≤ 200 characters each)

1. Smooth laser trail that fades by itself
2. Rough circles, boxes, triangles and arrows snap into clean shapes
3. Hold Alt for straight lines; Ctrl to keep a stroke freehand
4. Laser, Highlighter and Pen presets, plus up to eight of your own
5. Keep strokes on screen until you clear them; undo and redo
6. Right-click, scroll and slide clickers still reach the app underneath
7. Eight animated cursors in your laser colour
8. Solid, Glow and Neon styles in any colour
9. Every shortcut can be changed or turned off
10. Under half a megabyte; no CPU while idle; per-monitor scaling

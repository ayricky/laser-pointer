Does it work outside presentations?+

Yes. Draw over any app on Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11: a browser, editor, lesson or game. Strokes fade by themselves, or use Alt+K to keep them.

Why pay if it’s open source?+

The US$2.99 Microsoft Store version will use the same code, with signed builds, auto-updates and start with Windows. Paying supports Ricardo’s work. The free release remains available on GitHub.

Will it get in the way?+

Alt+L toggles the laser and Esc stops it. In the app, right-click passes through with a ripple and your mouse wheel scrolls the app beneath. There are no network connections or telemetry.

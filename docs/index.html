Default shortcuts

Rebind any shortcut in the app. In this demo, L also toggles the laser when your browser reserves Alt+L.

ActionShortcutActive
Turn laser on or offAlt+Lalways
Turn laser offEscwhile the laser is on
Undo last stroke or ClearCtrl+Zwhile the laser is on
RedoCtrl+Ywhile the laser is on
Clear strokesAlt+Xwhile strokes are on screen
Keep strokes on or offAlt+Kalways
Next / previous presetAlt+] / Alt+[always
QuitAlt+Shift+Lalways

While the laser is on: drag to draw, right-click to click through, scroll to scroll the app underneath, and Ctrl+wheel to change thickness.

Why pay for something open source?

The Store version uses the same code. You get a signed build, automatic updates and a clean way to start with Windows. Buying it funds development. Building it yourself is always free.

Does it send anything anywhere?

The app makes no network connections and collects no personal data. No telemetry, ads or accounts. Your settings stay on your PC.

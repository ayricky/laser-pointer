Laser Pointer

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Laser Pointer collects no personal information. This page explains what that means in practice.

What the app stores

Your settings — presets, colours, shortcuts and preferences — are saved in a file on your PC, %APPDATA%\LaserPointer\settings.ini, or in the app's own data folder when it's installed from the Microsoft Store. They never leave your PC. What you draw is held in the app's memory while it's on screen and for undo and redo, and is gone when the app closes. It is never saved, captured or recorded.

What it sends

Nothing. The app makes no network connections: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no account and no advertising.

Keyboard and mouse

The app listens for its own shortcuts, and while the laser is on it receives the mouse input over the screen so it can draw. It doesn't record keystrokes, and pointer movement becomes the strokes described above and nothing else.

Microsoft Store

If you buy the app from the Microsoft Store, the purchase is handled by Microsoft under Microsoft's privacy statement. Microsoft gives the developer sales figures and, if Windows reports a crash, the crash details it collected under that statement. The app itself sends nothing.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. If you switch between light and dark, your browser remembers the choice on your device; it isn't sent anywhere. GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/ayricky/laser-pointer.

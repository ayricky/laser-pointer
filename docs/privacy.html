Laser Pointer

Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Laser Pointer is a Windows presentation and annotation app by Ricardo Mariano.

No data collection

The app collects no personal data, makes no network connections, and has no telemetry, advertising or accounts. Your drawings stay on your screen; the app does not upload or share them.

Settings on your PC

Your preferences, presets and shortcuts are stored in %APPDATA%\LaserPointer\settings.ini. In packaged Store builds, Windows keeps the app's private copy of that folder. These settings are not sent to us.

Uninstalling the Store version removes its private settings. If you use a portable build, you can remove its settings by deleting the %APPDATA%\LaserPointer folder yourself.

Microsoft Store

Microsoft handles Store purchases, downloads and updates under its own privacy policy. The app does not receive your payment details.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so please avoid posting personal information.

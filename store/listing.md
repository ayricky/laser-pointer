## Full description

```text
Draw over anything while you present. Laser Pointer adds a smooth, fading laser to your Windows screen so you can point out a detail, circle an idea or guide your audience through a slide without changing applications.

Press Alt+L and drag to draw. The trail tapers smoothly and fades away. Choose Solid, Glow or Neon, pick a colour, and adjust thickness, opacity, fade time, trail length and smoothing.

Want your annotations to stay? Turn on Keep strokes or choose the Pen preset. Your drawings keep their original appearance even when you change presets, and stay visible when the laser is off. Undo and redo individual strokes. Clear fades the drawings away, and undo can restore a Clear.

Keep presenting without breaking your flow. Right-click passes a click through to the application underneath. The scroll wheel still scrolls; Ctrl+wheel changes thickness. A laser-dot cursor, click ripples and a brief on-screen indicator help your audience follow along.

Start with Laser, Highlighter and Pen, or save up to eight presets of your own. Rename, update or delete presets, switch them from the keyboard, and customise the shortcuts for every action.

The app lives in the Windows notification area. Left-click its icon for a tabbed settings panel with a live pen preview, light and dark themes, and your Windows accent colour. Optionally start it with Windows, ready for your next presentation.

Private by design: no personal data collection, network connections, telemetry, advertising or accounts. Settings stay on your PC.

Laser Pointer is open source under the MIT licence. The Microsoft Store version uses the same code, with signed distribution and automatic updates. Your purchase supports development; building it yourself is always free.

Requires Windows 10 version 1809 or later on an x64 PC. A mouse and keyboard are recommended. No subscription or in-app purchases.
```
